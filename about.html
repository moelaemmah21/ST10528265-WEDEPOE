About Lucky Answer Group

Certified. Local. Dependable.

Our Story

Lucky Answer Group was founded by a qualified electrician with years of hands-on experience in FTTH fault management and residential fibre coordination. Recognising that estates and homeowners were struggling to find one contractor who understood both electrical work and fibre networks, the business was built to close that gap: a single, reliable point of contact for electrical installations and fibre installation and maintenance.

Today, Lucky Answer Group serves residential estates, complexes, property developers and small businesses across Gauteng, with a growing team of qualified electricians and fibre technicians.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver safe, standards-compliant electrical and fibre installations, backed by dependable after-installation maintenance support, in line with South African fibre-optic cabling installation guidelines such as SANS 10198.

Our Vision

To be Gauteng's most trusted local name for combined electrical and fibre installation and maintenance services.

Certifications & Compliance

  • Registered Wireman's Licence
  • Registered with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PIRB) for estate access work
  • Certificate of Compliance (CoC) issuing electrician
  • FTTH installation practices aligned to SANS 10198 optical fibre cabling guidelines

Our Team

Our technicians are trained in both electrical installation work and FTTH fibre splicing, termination and fault-finding, allowing us to handle mixed electrical/fibre call-outs with a single site visit wherever possible.
