## Citation Format Reminder (Harvard, adapted for IIE)
For any image sourced externally, cite it in this format:

> Photographer/Creator Surname, Initial. (or Organisation Name), Year.
> *Title/description of image.* [online] Available at: <URL> [Accessed date].

Example:
> Pexels (Photographer Name), 2026. *Electrician working on distribution
> board.* [online] Available at: <https://www.pexels.com/photo/xxxxx/>
> [Accessed 13 August 2026].

All such citations must also be added to the main `README.md` References
section and the relevant page's in-text reference where the image appears,
per the PoE brief's referencing instructions.
