# Content Research and Sourcing — Lucky Answer Group

This document records where the text content for each page came from, as
required by section 3.1 of the WEDE5020 PoE brief.

## Organisation's Website / Social Media
Lucky Answer Group is a fictional (hypothetical) small business created for
this PoE, so it has no existing website or social media profiles to draw
content from. All organisational content below is therefore **original
content**, written specifically for this assignment, as permitted by the
brief ("if the storyline is fictional, you would still be required to
present realistic content for the fictional client").

## Original Content (written for this assignment)
- Company history, mission and vision statement (`about.html`)
- Service descriptions for electrical installations, FTTH installation and
  maintenance contracts (`services.html`, `index.html`)
- Branch contact details, addresses and enquiry/contact form copy
  (`contact.html`, `enquiry.html`)

  Note: physical addresses, phone numbers and email addresses used in
  `contact.html` are fictional placeholders created for demonstration
  purposes and do not belong to a real business.

## Public Domain / Industry Reference Sources
The following real, published sources were used to ground technical claims
made in the content (e.g. certifications, standards, and the rationale for
having a business website) in accurate, verifiable information:

- LeadServices, 2026. *Fiber optic installation for business in South
  Africa.* [online] Available at:
  <https://leadservices.co.za/blog/fiber-optic-installation-for-business/>
  [Accessed 13 August 2026]. — Used to reference SANS 10198 fibre-optic
  cabling standards and FTTH Council Africa membership mentioned on
  `about.html` and `services.html`.

- Small Businesses South Africa, 2026. *Why every small business needs a
  website.* [online] Available at:
  <https://smallbusinesses.co.za/why-small-business-needs-a-website/>
  [Accessed 13 August 2026]. — Informed the website goals and objectives
  described in the Website Project Proposal and README.

- Nielsen Norman Group (Whitenton, K.), 2020. *Navigation menus: 5 tips to
  make them visible.* [video] Available at:
  <https://www.nngroup.com/videos/navigation-menu-visibility/> [Accessed 13
  August 2026]. — Informed the consistent, always-visible navigation menu
  used across all five pages.

- Priority One, 2026. *Considerations for a small business website.*
  [online] Available at:
  <https://priorityone.co.za/considerations-for-a-small-business-website/>
  [Accessed 13 August 2026]. — Informed the decision to keep the site
  simple, fast, and focused on a clear call to action (quote requests).
